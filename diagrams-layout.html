Fubo Info

Provider Tools

Onboarding tips: submit your event schedule 30 days out, your rundown 72 hours out, graphics and video 7 days out, and book your 15-minute test signal at least 24 hours before each event. Each section saves on its own, so you can finish over days.

Supported devices: Fubo streams on Roku, Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV, Chromecast, iOS and Android phones and tablets, web browsers, and select smart TVs.

Testing & Layout

4K: select live events stream in 4K — you need a 4K-capable device and the right plan. Your contribution feed specs stay on the Spec Info page.

Test windows: book them on the Testing & Calendar page — the calendar shows your booked windows at a glance.

Access

Company history: Fubo was founded in 2015 by David Gandler and Alberto Horihue as a soccer-focused streaming service and went public on the NYSE in 2020. In 2025 it combined with Disney's Hulu + Live TV, with Disney holding a majority stake.
